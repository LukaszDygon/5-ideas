☠️
The Sovereign Rule: Normal Humans, Absurd Monster Lore
1. Guests bring their own costumes. Host hands them their assigned dossier cards on arrival.
2. The Corpse Penalty: Anyone who arrives without a costume is automatically sentenced to be the Deceased Victim of the night!
3. Stay In Character: Blend your mundane human neuroses (bills, air fryers, HOA gossip) with bloodcurdling gothic horror.

The Crypt Soirée

Arrival & Secret Briefing: Greet guests. If someone arrived costume-less, loudly read the Corpse Penalty Clause. Hand each guest their sealed Dossier Card.

💡 Prompt: "Mingle, grab a drink, and drop hints about your mundane job through your terrifying monster accent."
Act II: 25–35 Min

The Blackout & Crime

The Lights Go Out: Kill the lights (or trigger the sound effect!). A bloodcurdling scream echoes. Lights turn back on: the Penalty Victim is slumped dramatically on the floor!

🩸 Crime: Found slain near the snack table, clutching a torn note and dusted in mysterious powdered sugar!
Act III: 35–65 Min

The Monster Inquest

Interrogation & Clue Drops: Host reveals Evidence Cards #1 through #4 at 7-minute intervals. Guests question each other's ridiculous alibis and petty grudges.

🔎 Ghost Rule: The dead victim now lingers as a ghost, able to answer only in dramatic sighs and one-word whispers.
Act IV: 65–80 Min

The Grand Accusation

The Circle of Guilt: Each monster publicly points their claw/fang/wand at their prime suspect and states why. Host reveals the true killer & reads the confession!

🏆 Awards: Best Monster Voice, Worst Detective, and Most Dramatic Victim Death!
Host Script // Opening Speech (Read Aloud at Act I)

"Good evening, creatures of the night, damned souls, and Dave who apparently forgot what Halloween is. Welcome to the Annual Monster Gala. Tonight, we put aside our differences—the blood-feuds between vampires and werewolves, the ancient curses of the Nile, and that unresolved dispute regarding shared refrigerator etiquette.

Look around you. Every person in this parlor has a terrifying secret, an unpaid water bill, and an alibi thinner than single-ply toilet paper. Sip your cocktails, stay in character, and remember: if any one of you drops their monster persona, the curse will claim you next. Let the evening begin..."

CORPSE PENALTY
HOW TO ENFORCE: If 1 or more guests showed up without a monster costume, do NOT let them off easy. The first uncostumed arrival is designated Victim #1. Their job: socialize during Act I as a 'doomed mortal who thought this was just drinks', dramatically drop to the carpet in Act II, and spend Act III wearing a white napkin on their head as the Bitter Ghost of HR offering cryptic hints. If everyone wore costumes, assign the Victim role to an empty chair with a mannequin head or a volunteer!

CRIME SCENE EVIDENCE

The 4 Recovered Evidence Cards

Cut out or reveal sequentially during Act III at 7-10 minute intervals.

4 EXHIBITS LOGGED
EXHIBIT A: PHYSICAL TRACE REVEAL: MINUTE 35

Torn Silk Lining & Air Fryer Recipe Card

Found caught on the victim's belt buckle: a fragment of black velvet lining smelling faintly of mothballs, and a folded grease-stained index card titled "Keto Air-Fryer Brussels Sprouts (12 min at 190°C)".

🩸 Forensic Note: Who among our monsters is obsessed with low-carb suburban meal prep while pretending to subsist on the blood of virgins?
EXHIBIT B: TIMESTAMPTED ALIBI CRUSHER REVEAL: MINUTE 45

The Lidl Express Receipt (8:59 PM)

Found crumpled behind the radiator: a receipt time-stamped exactly 6 minutes before the blackout. Items purchased: 1x Garlic Hummus (Reduced to clear), 1x Roll of Heavy Duty Gauze, and 1x Can of Lynx Africa deodorant spray.

🩸 Forensic Note: The killer clearly had to make a last-minute emergency convenience store run before the murder.
EXHIBIT C: THE THREATENING STICKY NOTE REVEAL: MINUTE 55

The Post-It Note on the Fondue Pot

Stuck directly on the victim's half-empty punch glass: a neon pink sticky note written in frantic capital letters:
"STOP MICROWAVING SMOKED MACKEREL DURING CORE BUSINESS HOURS. THIS IS YOUR FINAL NOTICE BEFORE ETERNAL DAMNATION."

🩸 Forensic Note: Handwriting analysis indicates extreme HR corporate rage masking a gothic curse.
EXHIBIT D: THE MURDER WEAPON REVEAL: MINUTE 62

The Heavy Brass Fondue Skewer

Found dumped inside the umbrella stand by the front door: an antique brass fondue skewer coated in red sangria and melted gruyère. Wrapped around the handle is a rubber band printed with "Ergonomic Wrist Support Association 2024".

🩸 Forensic Note: The fatal blow required both proximity to the snack table and an intimate familiarity with office supply ergonomics!

THE KILLER UNMASKED

Suspect #1: Count Vlad of Human Resources

The Motive: The victim repeatedly microwaved leftover smoked mackerel in the breakroom microwave during Vlad's delicate coffin sleep window. Worse, the victim failed to submit their quarterly ergonomics assessment form, triggering an automatic corporate audit from corporate headquarters.

How the Clues Point to Vlad:
• Exhibit A: The air-fryer recipe card matches Vlad’s suburban obsession with low-carb garlic-free meal prep.
• Exhibit B: The Lidl receipt shows someone urgently buying garlic-free snacks and Lynx deodorant because their bat-breath failed them.
The Smoking Gun:
• Exhibit C: The Post-It note threatens eternal damnation over microwaved fish.
• Exhibit D: The fondue skewer was wrapped in an Ergonomic Wrist Support rubber band, issued exclusively to HR committee members!

The Culprit's Dramatic Confession (To be read aloud by Vlad):

"Fine! Yes! I did it! I impaled him with the fondue skewer! Have any of you tried sleeping 400 years in a cedar crypt while someone reheats oily mackerel on HIGH for four minutes?! It permeates the velvet lining! And don't get me started on the ergonomics! His lumbar support was an insult to the undead! I regret nothing! Now if you will excuse me, my sourdough starter needs feeding!"
