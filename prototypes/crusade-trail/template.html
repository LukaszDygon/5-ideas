Chronological Authorities & Source Materials Compiled from contemporary 11th & 12th century manuscripts

Geographic coordinates, campaign stops, and narrative excerpts follow primary eyewitness testimonies: the Anonymous Norman soldier's Gesta Francorum et aliorum Hierosolymitanorum, Raymond of Aguilers' Historia Francorum qui ceperunt Iherusalem, Albert of Aachen's Historia Ierosolimitana, and the imperial Byzantine perspective recorded in the Alexiad of Princess Anna Komnene. Modern long-distance pilgrim routes tracing this corridor include the international Jerusalem Way (Jerusalemweg) and the historical Via Militaris.
